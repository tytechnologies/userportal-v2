I.              SCOPE OF SERVICES

 

Effective upon signing hereof, the Property Management Company shall commence the performance of his task as Property Manager of {{property_address}} to wit:

 

 

1. General Administration

 

·         The Property Manager Administration shall submit his proposed Contract of Lease to the Owner for the latter’s approval; unless agreed upon in writing by the Owner, the terms and conditions of the Contract of Lease shall not be altered or amended by the Property Manager;

 

·         The Property Manager shall ensure compliance by the tenant with the provisions of the Contract of Lease;

 

·         The OWNER shall provide the keys of the property to the Property Manager for convenient access to the property for viewings, maintenance, cleaning, and necessary acts related to the management of the property.

 

 

2. Lease Administration

 

The Property Manager shall negotiate and sign in behalf of the Owner for all contracts of lease with tenants;

 

The Property Manager shall be the payee of, and receive rental payments and security deposits from the tenant and hold the same in trust for the Owner until the same is deposited in the account of the Owner.

 

The Property Manager shall make all necessary oral and written communications to tenants with regards to:

 

-           Contracts

-           Renovation Guidelines

-           Rules & Regulations

-           Violations

-           Maintenance

-           Move in clearances or forms

-           Move out clearance or forms

-           Gate Pass forms

-           All other items, forms or documents relevant to the occupancy of the condominium unit

 

The Property Manager shall implement any and all penalties within the legal bounds on any violations that the tenants may commit during the lease term;

 

The Property Manager shall implement proper termination or pre-termination procedures to tenants so as not to violate any legal rights of the tenant and not expose the Owner to possible liability; if necessary, he shall institute the appropriate court or legal action to implement the provisions of the contract of lease.

 

3. Exclusivity to Lease or to Sell:

 

·         To Offer the unit for Rent to any prospective client or co-broker.

·         To Assist the prospective clients or co-brokers in viewing the unit.

·         To Relay the Client’s or co-broker’s offer to the owner.

·         To Relay the Owner’s offer to the prospective client.

·         To Process any related documents related to the lease or sale of the property:

 

o   SAME CLAUSE and CONDITIONS as No. 2 Lease Administration of this agreement

 

 

II.            COMPENSATION/FEE OF THE PROPERTY MANAGER

 

The Owner hereby employs Property Management Company exclusively to lease, operate and manage said property subject to the terms and conditions of this agreement. In consideration of the management and leasing functions to be performed by Property Management Company under this agreement, Owner agrees to pay Broker a fee or fees for services rendered at the rates hereinafter set forth. Owner recognizes Property Management Company as agent in any negotiations relative to said property or any part thereof, which may have been initiated during the term hereof, and if consummated, shall compensate Broker in accordance with the rates hereinafter set forth. Such compensation is due and payable on demand and may be deducted by Property Management Company from gross receipts.

 

Management Fee:

 

Five percent (5%) of the monthly rental rate upon start of the lease contract

 

Leasing Fee:

 

One (1) month for a year lease or any amount surpassing the agreed rental and half month rental for renewals up to third year.

 

 

III.           TERM

 

The term of this Property Management Agreement shall be co-terminus with a tenant’s occupancy of the property but if there is no tenant yet, this agreement shall be effective for one (1) year lease starting from March 19, 2025 up to March 18, 2026, renewable upon mutual agreement of both parties. However, the Owner may pre-terminate this agreement in the event of non-performance by the Property Manager of his services outlined herein.
